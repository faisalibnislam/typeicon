# Drawing TypeIcon Core icons (for designers and agents)

TypeIcon Core is **original** artwork. Never copy, trace or paraphrase another icon set's geometry
(Font Awesome, Material, Tabler, Lucide, Phosphor, etc.). Draw from the object itself.

## Where things live
- `plan.json` (generated by `make_plan.py`): the concepts to draw, by category.
- `sets/<category>.py`: one module per category, icons defined with `dsl.py` (read its docstring first).
- Examples: `sets/math.py`, `sets/clothing.py`, `sets/body.py` and the v0.1 icons in `icons.py`.
- Spec: `docs/design/core-grid.md` (grid, styles, rules).

## Grid and craft rules
- 24 × 24 canvas, live area 2–22, stroke 2 px. Centre strokes on whole or half coordinates so edges land on pixels.
- Keyshapes: circle r 9, square 18 × 18, portrait 14 × 18, landscape 18 × 14. Keep optical weight consistent.
- Leave at least 2 px between separate strokes. No detail smaller than 2 px. Simplify: an icon is a symbol, not an illustration.
- Everything must read at 16 and 24 px. If it doesn't, remove detail.
- Prefer front or side views. Use 3/4 perspective only when the object is unrecognisable otherwise.

## The three styles (automatic from part roles)
- `shell(d)` for closed silhouettes: Line and Rounded stroke them; Filled makes them solid.
- `detail(d)` for lines inside a shell: Filled knocks them out.
- `line(d)` for open strokes: Filled makes them heavier.
- `dot`/`solid` for small solid marks.
- **Line must differ from Rounded.** Build polylines with `poly(..., r=S.r)` and rectangles with `rect(..., S.R)`
  (capped where the shape is small, e.g. `min(S.R, 2)`). Shapes made only of smooth curves (circles, ellipses,
  hearts, eyes) need an explicit difference: branch on `S.name` (e.g. a pointed tip for Line, a rounded tip for Rounded).
- If the automatic Filled design looks wrong, pass `filled=lambda: ...` to `@icon` and build it with
  `geometry.P / ST / U / D` (see the v0.1 icons in `icons.py`).

## Metadata
- `name`: lowercase kebab-case, ≥ 2 characters, from `plan.json`. Keep planned names. If a planned concept is a
  near-duplicate of an existing icon, skip it and say so in your report.
- `description`: one short sentence. `tags`: 3–8 search words (synonyms people type). `aliases`: only real alternative
  names (≥ 2 characters) that must type the icon in the font; they must not collide with any icon name.

## Workflow
1. Write 15–20 icons at a time into your category module(s).
2. Run: `.venv/bin/python tools/core-authoring/check.py sets/<category>.py --sheet build/review/<category>.png`
3. **Open the sheet image and look at every icon.** Fix anything unrecognisable, off-centre, too heavy or light,
   cramped, or inconsistent with its neighbours. Re-run until 0 problems.
4. Do not run `export.py` and do not edit files outside your own `sets/<category>.py` modules.
